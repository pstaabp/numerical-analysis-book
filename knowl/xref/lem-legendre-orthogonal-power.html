Lemma 6.5.2.

Let \(P_n(x)\) be the \(n\)the Legendre polynomial. Then
\begin{equation*} \int_{-1}^1 x^k P_n(x) \, dx = 0 \end{equation*}
for \(k=0,1,2,\ldots,n-1\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.

The function \(x^k\) can be written as
\begin{equation*} x^k = \sum_{j=0}^k b_j P_j(x) \end{equation*}
and therefore
\begin{equation*} \int_{-1}^1 x^k P_n(x) \, dx = \int_{-1}^1 \left(\sum_{j=0}^k b_j P_j(x) \right) P_n(x) \, dx = \sum_{j=0}^k b_j \int_{-1}^1 P_j (x) P_n(x) \end{equation*}
and since \(j \leq k \lt n\text{,}\) then the integral is 0 from orthogonality.
