Theorem 6.3.3.

If \(\{\phi_{0},\phi_{1},\phi_{2}, \ldots, \phi_{n}\}\) is an orthogonal set of functions on \([a,b]\) with respect to the weight function \(w\text{,}\) then \(n\)th degree polynomial
\begin{equation*} P_{n}(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{n} a_{k} \phi_{k}(x) \end{equation*}
minimizes \(||f-P_{n}||_{w}^2\) where for each \(k=0,1,2,\ldots, n\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} a_{k} = \frac{\langle f(x), \phi_k(x) \rangle_w}{ \langle \phi_k, \phi_k \rangle_w} = \frac{ \int_{a}^{b} f(x) w(x) \phi_{j} (x) \, dx}{\int_{a}^{b} w(x) \phi_{j}^{2} \, dx} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗

Proof.
Since \(\{\phi_{0},\phi_{1},\phi_{2}, \ldots, \phi_{n}\}\) is an orthogonal set of functions, then
\begin{equation*} \langle \phi_{i} , \phi_{j} \rangle = 0 \qquad \text{for all $i \neq j$.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Since
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned}E\amp = ||f-P_{n}||_{w}^2 = \biggl\vert\biggl\vert f(x) - \sum_{k=0}^{n} a_{k} \phi_{k}(x) \biggr\vert\biggr\vert^2 \\ \amp = \langle f(x) - \sum_{k=0}^n a_k \phi_k, f(x) - \sum_{k=0}^n a_k \phi_k \rangle \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
The minimum occurs when \(\partial E /\partial a_{j}=0\text{,}\) so
\begin{equation*} \begin{aligned} \frac{\partial E}{\partial a_{j}} \amp = \langle f(x) - \sum_{k=0}^n a_k \phi_k, - \phi_j \rangle_w + \langle - \phi_j, f(x) - \sum_{k=0}^n a_k \phi_k \rangle_w \\ \amp = -2 \langle f(x) - \sum_{k=0}^n a_k \phi_k, \phi_j \rangle_w = -2 \left( \langle f(x), \phi_j \rangle - \sum_{k=0}^n a_k \langle \phi_k, \phi_j \rangle\right) \\ \amp = -2 \left( \langle f(x), \phi_j \rangle - a_k \langle \phi_j, \phi_j \rangle \right) \end{aligned} \end{equation*}
where on the last step the fact of \(\langle \phi_k, \phi_j \rangle = 0\) when \(k \neq j\text{.}\)
🔗
Solving for \(a_j\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} a_j = \frac{ \int_{a}^{b} f(x) w(x) \phi_{j} (x) \, dx}{\int_{a}^{b} w(x) \phi_{j}^{2} \, dx} \end{equation*}
